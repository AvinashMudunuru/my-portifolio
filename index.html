CSIT STUDENT / AI EXPLORER / ANDHRA PRADESH

01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity, discipline, and a little experimentation.

I’m Avinash Varma Mudunuru, a first-year B.Tech student in Computer Science and Information Technology at Lendi Institute of Engineering and Technology. Based in Andhra Pradesh, I’m learning to connect programming fundamentals with the possibilities of AI.

I explore AI tools, build the tools I need, and experiment with interfaces that feel alive. Alongside classes and labs, I’m strengthening my mathematics, practising code, and building better study habits. I’m still at the beginning—and I want every small experiment to move me forward.

02 / THE EVERYDAY PRACTICE

Working principles

How I try to manage my time as a 1st year student to achieve my goals.

Attending classes regularly

I try not to miss my lectures and labs, since college is still new to me and I don’t want to fall behind in the first year itself.

Balancing studies and free time

I’m still learning how to manage my time between assignments, exams, and i am a bit slow in mathematics, on my free time i will work on linear algebra and calculus.

Learning the basics of coding

I’m new to programming, so I’m focusing on balancing and understanding the basics properly while rushing into advanced topics simultaneously.

Building good study habits

I’m trying to make small habits like revising notes and practicing problems regularly, instead of studying everything at the last minute.
